# Cover Letter and Email Rules

## Cover letter

- Add evidence or motivation not already obvious from the CV.
- Open with concrete relevance rather than generic enthusiasm.
- Connect one to three role requirements to supported examples.
- Explain why the role makes sense without inventing emotional attachment or company knowledge.
- Distinguish candidate facts from current company facts. Verify changing company facts from authoritative sources when research is requested.
- Keep the closing concise and professional.
- Stay within one page unless the user requests another format.

## Short application email

- State the target role and attached material immediately.
- Use one short relevance sentence supported by the candidate profile.
- Avoid reproducing the cover letter.
- Include a direct, neutral closing.

## Review

Check whether every paragraph contributes new information. Remove generic company praise, unsupported motivation, repeated CV bullets, exaggerated confidence, and symmetrical AI-like paragraph structure.
