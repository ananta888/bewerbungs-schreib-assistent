# CV and Resume Rules

## Default order

1. Name, target title, contact details, location, and relevant links
2. Professional summary in three to five lines
3. Logically grouped core skills
4. Reverse-chronological professional experience
5. Relevant projects not already evident from employment
6. Education
7. Certifications
8. Languages

## Content rules

- Make the first screen establish professional identity, strongest relevant experience, role fit, and evidence.
- Give the most relevant recent role three to six bullets. Shorten older or unrelated experience.
- Use `Action + Context + Result` when the result is supported.
- Omit unknown metrics. Suggest the metric as a question outside the document when it could strengthen the evidence.
- Vary verbs naturally without forcing synonyms.
- Avoid `Responsible for`, empty competence claims, biographies, dense paragraphs, and duplicated skill inventories.
- Keep employment dates internally consistent and preserve explainable chronology.
- Include projects only when they add relevant evidence.
- Keep bullets concise enough to scan, but retain context needed to understand scope.

## Review output

Report strong direct matches, transferable matches, missing or weak requirements, supported keywords worth adding, and keywords that remain unjustified.
