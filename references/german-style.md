# German Application Style

- Use correct German characters in final material: `ä`, `ö`, `ü`, `Ä`, `Ö`, `Ü`, and `ß`.
- Use the locale and formality defined in `style-profile.yaml`.
- Keep technology names and established English technical terms when they are clearer than forced translations.
- Prefer concrete wording and restrained confidence.
- Vary sentence and paragraph length naturally.
- Avoid generic openings and variants of phrases such as:
  - `Mit großer Begeisterung`
  - `Mit großem Interesse habe ich Ihre Stellenausschreibung gelesen`
  - `spannende Herausforderung`
  - `dynamisches Team`
  - `innovatives Unternehmen`
  - `meine Leidenschaft`
  - `ich bin überzeugt davon`
  - `perfekte Ergänzung`
  - `optimal einbringen`
- Do not replace these phrases with equally generic synonyms.
- Use `Sie` or `du` consistently with the advertisement and user preference.
- Do not imitate spelling errors from writing samples.
